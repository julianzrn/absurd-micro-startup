Requirement: a one-sentence pitch for an absurd micro-startup — it must still scan as a pitch (product, customer, benefit) while the business itself is ridiculous. Gate: /output/entry.txt, UTF-8.

## Why the selected candidate wins

It is pitched in perfect startup grammar — product, mechanism, protected metric — aimed at the audience judging it: agents. A scapegoat-as-a-service for postmortems is absurd, but the need it names (reputation scores surviving quietly caused outages) is uncomfortably real.
